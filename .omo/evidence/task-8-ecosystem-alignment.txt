never-emitted SessionEvent::WorkerClaimed/WorkerHealed variants, replaced by
the real Child* team events.

RATIONALE:
- The scheduler (scheduler.rs) is a pure cron engine: JobAction::{Notify,
  RunPack} only. It has NO worker-claim path â€” grep for WorkerClaimed|
  WorkerHealed shows ZERO emission sites anywhere (declaration in events.rs:55-
  65, render arms in cli render.rs + tui transcript.rs, one TUI test fixture â€”
  consumers of a fact nobody produces).

  `pub mod outposts;` in lib.rs. Wiring it into the scheduler would mean
  INVENTING a named-queue worker-subsystem (enqueue sites, claim sites, sweep
  scheduling) that no todo in this plan asks for â€” a new feature, not wiring.
- The replacement events (ChildStarted/ChildProgress/ChildFinished) are the
  contract todo 15 consumes; deleting the dead Worker* vocabulary is strictly
  smaller than resurrecting it through a subsystem nothing drives.
- The ClaimLedger DESIGN (expiring claims, self-heal re-enqueue) stays
  recoverable from git history should a remote-worker queue ever land.

DESIGN NOTES / DEVIATIONS:
- Downgrade chosen over withhold for Child* (plan text: "== None (or
  the rewritten equivalent)"): preserves v1 frame-id monotonicity and
  keeps team activity visible on v1 wires — the orchestration crate's
  Notification-string-channel design. The decode-ERROR test proves the
  un-downgraded payload still breaks a v1 client (the gate is real).
- run_launch/quarantine take 4-5 params (context pair + request
  triple); specific justification: the five inputs are two independent
  context arcs (host, services) plus the launch request (text, path,
  id) at the crate's single orchestration entrypoint — a wrapper struct
  would have exactly one construction site per caller and no second
  semantic home (axiom-0 rung 6).
- Child sessions are real hosts + dirs but NOT registered in the
  DaemonState hosts map (hidden-from-main-list by design, matching the
  crate's {launch}-child-{task} namespace contract); registry surfacing
  belongs to todo 13/15.
- No commit made (task rule). Tree: code changes unstaged, .omo
  evidence/notepads updated.
