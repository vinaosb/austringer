KEY DESIGN DECISION
-------------------
Handlers return PENDING outcomes (OpAction::Fail/Complete); run_operation applies them
AFTER the cancel check. Root cause found via a red test: applying Failed inside the raced
stage future let a mid-stage cancel find a terminal status (cancel record lost coherence).
Pending-application makes the cancel always win cleanly with a transitionable state.

EventLogWriter/AgentLoop can cross await points. No behavior change.
