RISKS / NOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The dispatcher is a pure resolution surface; daemon engine wiring
  (per-turn dispatch + observe + mediated confirmation UX) is the T40/T45
  composition step. The confirmation seam is the T9 identity contract,
  so wiring cannot bypass consent even if it wanted to.
- Benchmark rows are supplied by config today; the evals adapter (todo
  44) is the intended feeder of the ranking table. No live model is ever
  called from this crate (intel stays network-free, per todo 34).
- Chain walks are per-ORIGIN-model and positional by design (rank-verbatim
  forge semantics); cross-model chain hopping after landing on a hop is
  deliberately NOT implemented (deterministic, documented, reviewable).
- Wait-out windows clamp to min(server hint, configured, 75s); a server
  hint LONGER than the configured window waits the configured window,
  then retries - escalation to a confirmed ask happens only after the
  retry budget, never silently.
